𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 𝟏: 
𝐓𝐈𝐓𝐋𝐄 Begin with a working title that guides your research focus.

📝 𝐈𝐍𝐓𝐑𝐎𝐃𝐔𝐂𝐓𝐈𝐎𝐍 
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 2: Introduce your topic clearly and define any specific terminology used.
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 3: Emphasize why the topic is significant. What makes your research important?
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 4: Relate your work to existing knowledge. What’s already out there?
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 5: Identify the gap. What needs to be addressed or further investigated?
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 6: Formulate clear research questions to direct your inquiry.
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 7: Outline your purpose and objectives. What do you aim to achieve?

🔬 𝐌𝐄𝐓𝐇𝐎𝐃𝐒 
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 8: List your methodological steps. How will you approach your research?
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 9: Delve into the theory behind your chosen methodology.
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 10: Describe your experimental setup in detail.
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 11: Elaborate on the object of study - what are you examining closely?

📊 𝐑𝐄𝐒𝐔𝐋𝐓𝐒 
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 12: Provide a summary of your results. What have you found?
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 13: Compare different outcomes. How do they relate or differ?
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 14: Focus on your main discoveries. What stands out?
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 15: Address your research questions with your findings.

💡 𝐂𝐎𝐍𝐂𝐋𝐔𝐒𝐈𝐎𝐍 & 𝐃𝐈𝐒𝐂𝐔𝐒𝐒𝐈𝐎𝐍 
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 16: Support and defend your conclusions with evidence.
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 17: Explain conflicting results or any unexpected findings.
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 18: Reflect on the strength and significance of your findings.
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 19: Acknowledge the limitations of your study.
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 20: Highlight the importance and implications of your findings.
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 21: Establish how your work introduces new ideas or perspectives.

📄 𝐀𝐁𝐒𝐓𝐑𝐀𝐂𝐓 
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 22: Craft a concise abstract. It summarizes your paper.

🛠️ 𝐑𝐄𝐕𝐈𝐒𝐈𝐎𝐍 
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 23: Title vs. content alignment. Ensure the title reflects the content and findings.
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 24: Check that key terms are clear and consistent throughout the paper
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 25: Ensure your objectives and variables clear and relevant
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 26: Link all variables, techniques, and measurements to existing knowledge.
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 27: Confirm reproducibility i.e., Can someone else follow your footsteps and get the same results?
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 28: Check for repetition. Remove any redundancies.
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 29: Align with objectives. Confirm that all statements support your posed questions and objectives
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 30: Validate findings.
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 31: Ensure text coherence
STEP 32: Make an informative and standalone abstract

✨ 𝐏𝐎𝐋𝐈𝐒𝐇𝐈𝐍𝐆 
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 33: Use appropriate tenses
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 34: Verify all equations and mathematically correct and relevant
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 35: Explain any abbreviations used
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 36: Prune word usage. Avoid filler words that don't add value to your statements
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 37: Ensure all units and techniques are standardized
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 38: Assess the quality and relevance of figures/tables
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 39: Include all figures, tables and legends in text
𝐒𝐓𝐄𝐏 40: Ensure references are current

📚 Source: Hengl, t. and Gould, M., 2002. Rules of thumb for writing research articles.
